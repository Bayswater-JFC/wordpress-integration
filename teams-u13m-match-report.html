Round 1

vs East Burwood - April 19 2025

🟡🟢 Round 1 Match Report 🟢🟡

It was great to kick off the season at our home ground playing against East Burwood. Our first official game running out as a newly formed team and with some new faces to the club. It was great to see Alex, Blake and Shane play their first game for the Baysie footy club.

Prior to the game we spoke about setting clear team goals for the season ahead being to have fun, build positive relationships and to improve as individuals and as a team.

It was a tough match up to start with considering we're still getting to know each other, each other's style of play, and getting used to the game plan. But throughout the course of the game we saw some glimpses of what we want to work toward. This was highlighted by a great passage of play, with Ben running off half back for a handball receive which led to quick transition, followed by a great contested mark by B who quickly got it on the boot to find Josh B in space to get our first goal of the season.

We saw Shane impact the contest with some strong moments of play, with some big tackles and bursts through the pack, this really stood out in the last quarter where he had 3 or 4 players hanging off him but kept pushing himself forward towards the goal. Jackson was huge in this game as well with his unquestionable intensity for the contest, laying some huge tackles and working hard all game.

We also took the opportunity in this game to try players in a few different positions to gain a better understanding of what our best lineups and positioning might be for the season. Hamish, Jacob and Chase all had a go in the ruck and each had moments where they showed some great capability, and most players had a chance to play in the forward and backline in this game.

A big thanks to Dan Robb for being our runner, Heather, James and Jess for keeping us organised and Luke for taking care of the kids, thank you all for your support.

Thanks to our parent helpers on the day, Alex, Kayden, Mitchell, Chase and Jack’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

Whilst the result wasn’t how we’d hoped to start the season, Gordon and I were really impressed with the spirit in which the team played the game and how every player left the field with their heads up and remaining positive. We’ll get to work at training on some of the things we identified in this game to help us to achieve our team goal of continuing to improve.

Go Baysie.

Round 2

vs Kilsyth - April 26 2026

🟡🟢 Round 2 Match Report 🟢🟡

After an exciting week for our team where we announced our first ever leadership team, we were ready for round 2 up against Kilsyth at their home ground. A huge congratulations to Ben on being named team Captain, Brodie on being named Vice Captain and Flynn, Hamish, Jacob, Josh B and Shane for being named as leaders of our team.

Also, what an exciting game for us celebrating two 50 game players. Congratulations to Blake and Max on this fantastic achievement, even though it’s only been a short time you have both been fantastic to coach and a pleasure to have as part of the team. It was so exciting to see you both run through your banner and lead the team on to the field after we acknowledged your achievement pregame with a milestone medal presentation. A huge thank you to Andrew for being involved as well and sharing some words for the boys as part of this.

It was a great team game, played in the spirit of the ANZACs as we saw great teamwork and hard work ethic throughout the game. Congratulations to B on being awarded the ANZAC medal by the Kilsyth coach as the best and fairest player on the ground for our team.

I can’t remember a time where we saw every player be so involved and have an impact on the game. This is a testament to the team's attitude towards team first play and the effort which every player put into the game. Who knew Chase could snap a goal that well? And twice mind you! Mitchell was everywhere and finished with a lazy 5 snags playing mostly off the wing, and Alex just kept going whilst taking some huge hits in this game, sticking at it to finish with 3 goals of his own. However, what Gordon and I were most thrilled to see was the efforts in the contest, with some great spoils, big contested marks and some great chases to close them down when in space, fantastic effort by all!

A huge thank you to our team officials who give up their time every week and to our parent helpers on the day, Flynn, Angus, and Jackson’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

It was great to sing the song as a team (even though some of you don’t know the words yet), and celebrate our 50 gamers Blake and Max with them getting a light squirt of water outside. 

Great win team, well done!

Go Baysie!

Round 3

vs North Ringwood - May 3 2026

🟡🟢 Round 3 Match Report 🟢🟡

We came into our round 3 game against North Ringwood expecting this to be a rough and tough one for us after our previous encounters with them, and it certainly proved to be. We didn't let that get to us though and fought hard throughout the game, we took some big knocks but we kept getting back up and fighting.

We thought Hamish really came into his own in this game having a real advantage in the ruck contest and getting first hands on the ball. Unfortunately, we weren’t able to capitalise on this by getting the ball going our way out of the middle. We saw some of our players starting to really lift late and we started to control the contest in patches, but after B took one hit too many and came off with a suspected concussion we were down and out for the day.

Despite the loss, we still showed some signs of improvement in our game. There were a number of passages of play where we controlled the ball in congestion with a quick handball out to a player in space, but again we just couldn't get the ball going our way effectively.

A huge thank you to our team officials who give up their time every week and to our parent helpers on the day, Hunter, Blake, Shane, Alex and Jackson’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

We’ll get back into training and work on some areas that we know will help us to improve and continue to grow throughout the season.

Go Baysie!

Round 5

vs Beaconsfield - May 17 2026

🟡🟢 Round 5 Match Report 🟢🟡

Needing to respond after a couple of off weeks, we were up against the benchmark team Beaconsfield in round 5 and missing some important players, so we knew we were in for another tough game. Whilst we didn’t get the win this time, it was a good opportunity to get a sight of the top opposition in our grade to know what areas we can work on to get them by the end of the season.

Round 6

vs Noble Park - May 24 2026


🟡🟢 Round 6 Match Report 🟢🟡

We were away against Noble Park in round 6, and after a few tough weeks, and still missing some crucial players we were confident that we could get the win. And wowee we certainly did that! Easily the highest score and winning margin in our teams history and I think the first time we’ve ever held an opposition team scoreless, with a massive 33 goals 20 points 218, that’s right 218!! to zero win!

Congratulations to Josh O (with 5 mind you), Blake, Luke, Jack and Anmar all kicking their first goals for Baysie!

Gordon and I were absolutely thrilled with the way we played as a team in the first half of this game. The way we moved the ball into space, using effective handballs to create and get the ball moving forward. To start the game we had moved a couple of players around to great effect with Kayden playing the wing role to perfection and Mitchell adding some much needed class and attacking play at half forward. The unselfish play shown by some of our players early in the game was just wonderful to see with handballs over the top often to let others have a shot at goal.

We did however lose shape a little after half time and with most of our players getting a sniff of a goal and pushing forward which started to clog up our forward line and slowed our scoring in the second half. But all in all, there were so many positives to take away from this game. The leading of the forwards which we’ve really been working on at training started to shine through and our wings held their positioning really well to create space and allow us to move the ball more freely.

A huge thank you to our team officials who give up their time every week and to our parent helpers on the day, Kayden, Mitchell, Flynn, and Shane’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

It was great to sing the song as a team again, it looked like everyone is starting to know the words now. Well done team.

Go Baysie!
