Round 1

vs East Burwood - April 19 2025

🟡🟢 Round 1 Match Report 🟢🟡

It was great to kick off the season at our home ground playing against East Burwood. Our first official game running out as a newly formed team and with some new faces to the club. It was great to see Alex, Blake and Shane play their first game for the Baysie footy club.

Prior to the game we spoke about setting clear team goals for the season ahead being to have fun, build positive relationships and to improve as individuals and as a team.

It was a tough match up to start with considering we're still getting to know each other, each other's style of play, and getting used to the game plan. But throughout the course of the game we saw some glimpses of what we want to work toward. This was highlighted by a great passage of play, with Ben running off half back for a handball receive which led to quick transition, followed by a great contested mark by B who quickly got it on the boot to find Josh B in space to get our first goal of the season.

We saw Shane impact the contest with some strong moments of play, with some big tackles and bursts through the pack, this really stood out in the last quarter where he had 3 or 4 players hanging off him but kept pushing himself forward towards the goal. Jackson was huge in this game as well with his unquestionable intensity for the contest, laying some huge tackles and working hard all game.

We also took the opportunity in this game to try players in a few different positions to gain a better understanding of what our best lineups and positioning might be for the season. Hamish, Jacob and Chase all had a go in the ruck and each had moments where they showed some great capability, and most players had a chance to play in the forward and backline in this game.

A big thanks to Dan Robb for being our runner, Heather, James and Jess for keeping us organised and Luke for taking care of the kids, thank you all for your support.

Thanks to our parent helpers on the day, Alex, Kayden, Mitchell, Chase and Jack’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

Whilst the result wasn’t how we’d hoped to start the season, Gordon and I were really impressed with the spirit in which the team played the game and how every player left the field with their heads up and remaining positive. We’ll get to work at training on some of the things we identified in this game to help us to achieve our team goal of continuing to improve.

Go Baysie.

But all in all, there were so many positives to take away from this game. The leading of the forwards which we’ve really been working on at training started to shine through and our wings held their positioning really well to create space and allow us to move the ball more freely.

Round 8

Round 8 vs Lilydale - June 14 2026

🟡🟢 Round 8 Match Report 🟢🟡

We were back at our home ground for our round 8 match against Lilydale, with some extra excitement and anticipation for our planned event after Round Up to complete an Ice Bucket race against the U17 Boys in support of Fight MND.

After the week off we looked to be a little off early, lacking a bit of attack at the contest and effort around the ground, but we still maintained field position leading to lots of scoring opportunities. We were very wasteful though, kicking 1 goal 7 behinds in the first quarter and with poor execution moving the ball inside our forward 50 we turned the ball over far too frequently by kicking straight to the opposition.

In the second quarter Lilydale started to get on top of us with a quick burst of 3 goals, they moved the ball quickly with precision and did not waste their opportunities, hitting their targets when entering their forward 50 and converting. We looked in real trouble, but were able to maintain the lead through this period and what was really impressive, after their third goal, we immediately took control of the contest to stop their momentum and run away with the game.

We still lacked polish, finishing with a final score of 13 goals 20 behinds and we continued to turn the ball over throughout the game, however the positive is that we were able to maintain complete control and finish the game really strong after being challenged. We will continue to work on our forward craft at training as once again we felt that we clogged up our forward line too much and didn't protect the ‘danger zone’ and create space.

A huge thank you to our team officials who give up their time every week and to our parent helpers on the day, Blake, Shane, Jack, Josh O and Mitch’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

It was great to sing the song as a team again and take part in a special night with our Ice Bucket race. Incredible effort by you all to get behind a great cause and to contribute to raising $8037 as a club for FightMND is just incredible. I’m so proud of the team for coming together and showing some great community spirit. The event itself was so much fun and I hope you all enjoyed it as much as I did.

Go Baysie!

Whilst we didn’t get the win, we showed some real heart and real grit in the last quarter which is the type of play that will hold up in finals footy.
