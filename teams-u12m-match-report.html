Round 1

vs Beaconsfield - April 6 2025

🟡🟢 Round 1 Match Report 🟢🟡

We kicked off the season against Beaconsfield on a beautiful sunny day for footy. And what an exciting way to start by celebrating two 50 game players. Congratulations to Hamish and Anmar on this fantastic achievement, you have both been fantastic to coach over the years and a pleasure to have as part of the team. It was so exciting to see you both run through your banners and lead the team on to the field after we acknowledged your achievement pregame with a milestone medal presentation. A huge thank you to Shaun for being involved as has become tradition on these occasions, and sharing some words for the boys as part of this.

To add to the occasion Hamish was our captain for the game after celebrating his birthday on the 5th. With both teams a little short for numbers we were playing a 16 v 16 game. This gave us an opportunity to consider some different tactics in how we would set up our forward and back lineups. We made a decision to run without a centre half forward to open up space for our mids to run through and our full forward to lead up. It was so exciting to see off the very first ball up our 3 mids Brodie, Caleb and Levi F streaming forward into that space sharing the ball between them.

We didn’t stop there, dominating the first quarter with 7 goals 2 behinds to no score for Beaconsfield. This provided us the opportunity to switch players around to try a few different things and to give players the opportunity to get some experience in different positions.

By half time the game was won so we changed our tactics again and moved our 50 gamers into the forward line with the plan to try and get them each a goal. We were able to help Hamish slot one after just missing earlier with a poster, and provided Anmar with a few opportunities but in tricky situations for him to convert.

We were absolutely thrilled to see what we have been working on this preseason start to come to life in the game. Players were clearly looking to find teammates in a better position and using handballs to move the ball while maintaining control. This and the clear intention to play team first footy really stood out and Gordon and I are very proud of the team.

A big thanks to Dan Robb for being our runner, Julie for keeping us organised and Luke for taking care of the kids, thank you all for your support.

Thanks to our parent helpers on the day, Levi T, Emmalee and Aadhi’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

It was great to sing the song as a team, with our 50 gamers Hamish and Anmar in the middle of the circle getting a light squirt of water. I’m looking forward to the chance to celebrate further milestones with the team this season. Great win team, well done!

Go Baysie.

Round 2

vs Croydon - April 27 2025

🟡🟢 Round 2 Match Report 🟢🟡

After a few weeks break for school holidays and Easter, we were back for round 2 against Croydon. It was a great team game, played in the spirit of the ANZACs as we saw great teamwork and hard work ethic throughout the game.

Our captain for the game was Caleb who led the team well for this special round. Congratulations also to Caleb on being awarded the ANZAC medal by the Croydon coach as the best and fairest player on the ground for our team.

It was a really hard fought game and there didn’t seem to be much separating the teams throughout. After a sluggish start in the first quarter we seemed to be in control but just not taking our opportunities when in control of the ball. After discussing this at quarter time there was a shift right away with us taking care of the ball when in possession and moving the ball in a controlled way to ensure we were able to score well and we opened up a nice lead. By three quarter time we had a nice 5 goal lead so changed a few things around to continue to learn different roles for the team. Croydon finished really strong in the last quarter and came back well. We still managed to get a nice win by a couple of goals and learnt a little bit about our team in the last.

A big thank you to Josh O, who even though he couldn’t be out there playing he still did his part for the team helping me in the coaches box as Assistant Coach for the game in Gordon’s absence.

Thanks to our parent helpers on the day, Josh O, Levi F, and Archer’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

It was great to sing the song as a team after the win, let’s keep working on playing well as a team and continuing to improve as the season goes on.

Go Baysie!

Round 3

vs Rowville Knights - May 4 2025

🟡🟢 Round 3 Match Report 🟢🟡

Round 3 we were up against the Rowville Knights, and it was another special game with us celebrating the 50 game milestone for Levi T, congrats Levi what a fantastic achievement! We were also fortunate to celebrate a new player with Lincoln playing his first game for the club, well done and welcome to Lincoln and his family.

We had 2 experienced players captaining us this week and knew we’d be in good hands with Jacob and Josh B leading the way. Being a little short on numbers again this week, we had Randall and Phoenix filling in, thank you both you certainly didn’t look out of place playing up a couple of age groups and had some great moments throughout the game.

Whilst it wasn’t our best game this week, we certainly continue to show that we are up to the level after moving up a grade this season. It was close the whole way through and even on an off day, we stuck at it to only lose by a point. There were times in the game where we thought we didn’t have our usual intensity, but reflecting on this Gordon and I have been considering over the last couple of weeks making more rotations, particularly through the middle of the ground with longer quarters this season. We’ll try to adjust as we go through the season to ensure we’re setting the players up to be their best.

Despite the loss, we still showed some great signs of improvement in our game. There were a number of passages of play where we controlled the ball in congestion with quick handballs and some great plays of moving the ball to players in space with kicking and marking. When we asked for a lift in intensity we saw some great moments of those one percenters, with shepherding improving throughout the game and a real match turner of a moment with Angus laying a huge chase down tackle in the forward pocket and earning the free.

Overall we are very proud of the kids, particularly the manner in which they play the game, they really are a great bunch. They should all hold their heads high knowing that we have been very competitive to start the season and I’m sure as the season goes on other teams will see exactly what we’re capable of.

Thanks to our parent helpers on the day, Ben, Levi F and Caleb’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

We play our first home game for the season next week against our main rival from last season, so let’s reset and get ready to turn up strong for a big game.

Go Baysie!

vs North Ringwood - June 1 2025

🟡🟢 Round 7 Match Report 🟢🟡

Our round 7 match was away against North Ringwood. With the competition seeming to be very even this season we knew it was going to be a tough game, particularly with North Ringwood being a strong opponent in the past. 

We were led out by our captain Josh O, and got off to a strong start holding onto a close lead at the end of the first quarter. Hamish was admirable on the last line of defense playing a really strong game and putting pressure on to cause them to miss some goals that they probably expected to kick, well done Hamish.

Congratulations to Jackson on playing his first game. Once he got out there he showed some real speed and great attack on the contest. I’m really excited to see what he can add to our team. Welcome Jackson and family, it’s great to have you as part of the club.

We were led out by Emmalee and Lincoln as captains in what turned out to be a very competitive and well played game. Each team had their moments and although East Burwood got away from us a little, in the end it was a much closer match than what the score might suggest and we definitely showed glimpses of what our best footy can be.

We shuffled the magnets a little in this game to accommodate some injuries and illness, and it was great to see players who changed roles, taking the opportunity and showing a lot of potential. Wes was having a fantastic game after being shifted behind the ball in Ben’s absence due to illness, and was playing an absolute ripper game until being injured and unfortunately having to sit out the rest of the game. Brodie took an opportunity to get back in the middle and added some real dash and dare which has been missing from his game this season but it was exciting to see it back. Jack took responsibility up forward with Wes changing roles and showed some glimpses of what he can bring to the team, getting to really dangerous positions on the goal side of the opposition.

A huge thank you to Randall for helping us out and filling in today, he had a huge impact on the game particularly early on and we thought he might have been best on up until half time. A shout out to Levi for getting out on the field, despite facing some discomfort with his injuries he was still willing to get out there and help the team as much as he could, what fantastic team spirit, well done Levi!

A huge thank you to our team officials who give up their time every week and to our parent helpers on the day, Levi F, Angus, Levi T, Josh O and Mitch’s families for helping to ensure our kids get to play and enjoy the game.

We may not have got the result we were after in the game, but this round was certainly one of the best memories I’ve had as part of this team. The incredible effort of you all to get behind a cause and raise $3793 for FightMND is just incredible. I’m so proud of the team for coming together and showing some great community spirit. The event itself was so much fun and I hope you all enjoyed it as much as I did.

Go Baysie!
