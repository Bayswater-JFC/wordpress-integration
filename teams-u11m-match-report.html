First game of the season and the energy was high from the very first bounce! The boys were keen and eager to get involved, attacking the contest hard, laying tackles, and supporting each other all over the ground.

A strong start to the season with plenty to build on — we’ll take the positives and come back even better next week!

The U11’s played one of their most determined games of the season on Saturday, falling just short in a thrilling contest against Blackburn by only 4 points.

The game was played at a high intensity from start to finish, with both teams attacking the contest hard and refusing to give an inch. Our players responded brilliantly to the challenge, showing great composure with the ball and plenty of grit when the game tightened up late.

What was especially pleasing was the way the group handled momentum swings throughout the match. Each time Blackburn pushed, the boys answered back with effort and teamwork. There were several passages of play where the ball movement, communication and defensive pressure were exactly what we’ve been working on at training.

Oliver, Logan, Ryley, Alyan and Lewis were all outstanding contributors across the four quarters. Their attack on the footy, willingness to compete and leadership around teammates had a real impact on the game.

While it was disappointing to miss out on the win in the final minutes, there was so much to like about the performance. The resilience, sportsmanship and trust the players showed in one another continue to demonstrate how much this group is growing together as a team.

A big thank you for all the encouragement from the sidelines never goes unnoticed by the kids or coaches.

The effort is there, the improvement is clear, and this team is building something really exciting.
