Round 1

Vs Montrose
Welcome to the season, today we finally got to step out onto the field and put all our hard preseason work into action. It was a debut game for Megan and Kira, and also debut club games for Tyla, Ruby and Charlie. 

It wasn’t quite the result we had hoped as we came up against a very experienced Montrose outfit. I couldn’t fault the effort and intensity of all of our players which has been a focus in the preseason. The tackling pressure on point and we were rewarded with multiple holding the ball decisions to give ourselves the best chance to move the ball forward. Voice and communication (which was another focus of pre season) was exceptional and heard right across the field. Thanks to all the parents who helped out on duties, looking forward to round 2. 

Best: Keely, Raelene, Shaylee, Megan, Jade.  Goals: -

Round 2

Round 3

R3 v Blackburn

Bayswater 9.11.65

Blackburn Red 4.0.24

This week saw us take on Blackburn on a perfect autumn afternoon at home. Right from the first bounce you could see that the girls were focused and committed. The tackling pressure was immense. We used the wind to our advantage and managed to put 3 goals on the board early. The second quarter was a bit of a see-saw with both teams scoring goals. In the third we again used the wind to our advantage and I managed to lock the ball in our forward line for most of the quarter. The wing players Carly Charlie Lily and Ruby setup behind the ball well. This allowed multiple shots on goal and we kicked 4 for the quarter. The last quarter was a bit quieter with Blackburn scoring 2 goals. Everyone played their roles and gave 100% effort 100% of the time. 

Best : Shaylee, Shayla, Fenix, Jade, Jess, Matilda, Carly, Ruby Mac

Goals : Shayla 3, Fenix 3, Shaylee 1, Ella 1, Jess 1
