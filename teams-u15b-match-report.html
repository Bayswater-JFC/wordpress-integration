Round 1

Today we played Wantirna South at their ground for Round One of the season. Hoping we would get off to a good start.
The first quarter wasn't great at all. Our effort at the ball, our talk and the want to pick up players wasn't good enough so the other team controlled the first quarter. We had a good chat at quarter time to try and change a few things. We were better in the second quarter but the other team was still playing better than us. The third quarter was a bit the same but things were starting to change and we were getting a lot closer to the other teams level. They only out scored us by a goal in this quarter.
The last quarter was really good. We put pressure on the opposition, when we got the ball we kicked it long and players up the field put themselves in the right spots to give us the best chance to win the ball. We ended out scoring them in the last quarter which was very pleasing to see that we never gave up. We did some things pretty well by the end of the game and there are some things we will work on to improve next week.
We lost 99-53.

Round 2

Today was Anzac round. A game where everyone wants to show their fighting spirit.

Unluckily for us, Montrose were able to get a few late goals to give them a good lead at quarter time. The next three quarters were pretty even. Round 3

The forecast was for lots of rain but lucky for everyone, we only got a little rain during the game.
